## Manual early-source checks

Four historical technology dates were checked against earlier original or official documentation. These dates are not claims of verified first-ever exact wording.

- **AIH-14501 model view controller** — 1979, [primary source](https://zenodo.org/records/3676092). Original December 1979 Xerox PARC MVC technical note. MVC idea existed in 1978; the original note is an early documented method source, not proven earliest wording.
- **AIH-15481 mamdani** — 1975, [primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0020-7373(75)80002-2). January 1975 original fuzzy linguistic controller paper. The later use of the eponym 'Mamdani' for a fuzzy inference type is not proven to originate in 1975.
- **AIH-15691 border gateway protocol** — 1989, [primary source](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc1105). June 1989 RFC 1105 original BGP protocol specification. This is an early primary-source occurrence, not independently certified first-ever naming.
- **AIH-16010 ethereum** — 2013, [primary source](https://ethereum.org/ethereum-forks/). Ethereum.org's official history records the introductory whitepaper on 27 November 2013. The maintained version of the whitepaper is labeled 2014, and the Ethereum network launched in 2015. This is a dated project-introduction milestone, not an AI research term.
