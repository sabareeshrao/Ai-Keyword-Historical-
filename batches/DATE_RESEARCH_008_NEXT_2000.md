## Human research source audit

Nine historically important terms were corrected to original or early scholarly sources; two unrelated cross-domain citations were rejected. All original IDs remain unchanged.

- AIH-08508 **Masked Autoencoder (MAE)**: 2021 ([research source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06377)). November 2021 first public preprint of the named scalable vision MAE architecture. This does not prove earliest use of generic masked autoencoding.
- AIH-08509 **Position Interpolation**: 2023 ([research source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595)). Original June 2023 paper on RoPE-based LLM Position Interpolation. Previous 1949 match was unrelated to this long-context technique.
- AIH-08510 **WordNet**: 1990 ([research source](https://doi.org/10.1093/ijl/3.4.235)). 1990 International Journal of Lexicography documentation of the WordNet lexical database. Earlier project work may have existed; 1990 is a documented publication, not proven coinage.
- AIH-08513 **Matryoshka Embedding**: 2022 ([research source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13147)). May 2022 Matryoshka Representation Learning preprint, introducing variable-dimensional Matryoshka representations. Term variant 'Matryoshka Embedding' may have appeared later.
- AIH-08515 **WordPiece**: 2012 ([research source](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP.2012.6289079)). 2012 ICASSP paper describing word-piece-based segmentation for Japanese and Korean voice search. Early published use, not independently established first-ever terminology.
- AIH-09002 **TIES Merging**: 2023 ([research source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01708)). Original June 2023 research preprint introducing TIES-Merging. Replaces an indirect later bibliographic hit.
- AIH-09015 **KL Divergence**: 1951 ([research source](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729694)). Original 1951 Kullback-Leibler information divergence paper; does NOT establish first use of the precise later expression 'KL divergence'.
- AIH-09502 **Optical Flow**: 1980 ([research source](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/e7968bf6-651d-4fd0-b4e8-c608756f2bba)). April 1980 MIT Artificial Intelligence Laboratory technical report AIM-572; predates 1981 journal publication. Optical flow as a broader concept predates this particular method.
- AIH-10008 **Top-p**: 2019 ([research source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09751)). Original April 2019 nucleus sampling paper; modern top-p text decoding derives from this method. Date is not proven first use of literal string 'Top-p'.
- AIH-08516 **Feedback Loop**: old date withdrawn. 1978 paper on plant stomata and carbon-dioxide feedback, not a source for AI feedback loops.
- AIH-10002 **Tool Use**: old date withdrawn. 1987 archaeology book on prehistoric stone-tool use, not AI agent tool use.
