## Semantic source audit

Five earlier technical sources corrected, and two unrelated source matches withdrawn. These are dated milestones, not proven coinages of exact words.

- **AIH-18510 sequence diagram**: 1997 ([early source](https://www.omg.org/spec/UML/1.1/About-UML)). OMG adopted UML 1.1 in December 1997, documenting UML sequence diagrams. This replaces an unrelated 1966 source; other non-UML sequence-diagram terminology may predate UML.
- **AIH-18511 sequence diagrams**: 1997 ([early source](https://www.omg.org/spec/UML/1.1/About-UML)). OMG UML 1.1 was adopted December 1997 and formally describes sequence diagrams. Replaces an unrelated human-factors 1961 title; NOT first-ever use of phrase.
- **AIH-18522 software product quality**: 1982 ([early source](https://doi.org/10.1109/AFIPS.1982.74)). AFIPS National Computer Conference paper (1982), pages 393–398; DOI itself contains 1982. The earlier imported 1899 year is a bibliographic metadata error. This is early technical evidence, not proven first use.
- **AIH-20004 network codes**: 2000 ([early source](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.850663)). July 2000 foundational network coding method paper, describing coding at network nodes. This is the method's introduction era; the plural phrase network codes may have earlier meanings.
- **AIH-20100 perfect secrecy**: 1949 ([early source](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1949.tb00928.x)). Shannon's October 1949 published perfect-secrecy theory; paper notes a confidential predecessor dated September 1, 1945. This is a foundational publication rather than proof of first expression use.
- **AIH-19508 co-operative systems**: unrelated dated source withdrawn. 1943 generic co-operative systems paper lacks a specific AI-infrastructure or computing connection.
- **AIH-19017 power outage**: unrelated dated source withdrawn. 1961 power-utility report does not establish the origin of a Speech & Audio technology term.
